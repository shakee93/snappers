# Return Policy

At Catlitter, we want you and your pets to be happy with every order. If something is not right, please read the policy below so we can help you as quickly as possible.

## Eligible Returns

You may request a return or exchange when:

- The item arrived **damaged** or **defective**.
- You received the **wrong product** or an **incorrect quantity**.
- The product is **unopened**, in its **original packaging**, and still **sealed** where applicable.

Return requests must be made within **7 days** of delivery. Items must be unused and in resalable condition unless the return is due to damage, defect, or our error.

## Non-Returnable Items

For hygiene and safety reasons, the following cannot be returned once opened or used:

- Opened **pet food**, **treats**, or **supplements**
- Opened **litter** or consumable refills
- Items marked as **final sale** or **clearance**
- Products with a **broken seal**, missing label, or visible signs of use
- Gift cards or promotional vouchers

If you receive a damaged or incorrect consumable product, contact us before opening or using it so we can arrange a replacement or refund.

## Damaged or Incorrect Orders

If your order arrives damaged, incomplete, or incorrect:

1. **Inspect your delivery** before the rider leaves whenever possible.
2. Take **clear photos** of the product, packaging, and shipping label.
3. [Contact us](/contact) within **48 hours** of delivery with your order number and photos.

We will arrange a **replacement**, **exchange**, or **refund** at no extra delivery cost when the issue is confirmed on our side or during transit.

## How to Request a Return

To start a return or exchange:

1. [Contact us](/contact) with your **order number**, the **item name**, and the reason for the return.
2. Wait for our team to confirm eligibility and next steps.
3. Return approved items in their **original packaging** with all accessories and labels intact.

Do not send items back without confirmation — unauthorized returns may not be accepted.

## Refunds

Once we receive and inspect a returned item:

- Approved refunds are processed within **5–7 business days**.
- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used at checkout.
- For **Cash on Delivery** orders, refunds are made via **bank transfer** to an account you provide.

Delivery charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

## Exchanges

If you need a different size, flavour, or variant, contact us within **7 days** of delivery. Exchanges are subject to stock availability. Any price difference will be charged or refunded accordingly.

## Late or Missing Deliveries

If your order has not arrived within the estimated delivery window, [contact us](/contact) and we will follow up with the courier on your behalf.

## Questions

For any return-related questions, please [contact us](/contact) and our team will be happy to assist.
